# Responsive design

Below 768px, claim tables become vertical cards with claim number, product, status, date, and action. Admin cards also show customer and AI priority. Search and filters stack at full width; drawers become full width; the login screen becomes a single column.

At 768–1023px, spacing and card gaps tighten while retaining the larger layout. At 1024px and above, dashboard statistics and the claim table use the full content width. No sidebar or horizontal table scroll is required for the primary mobile workflow.
